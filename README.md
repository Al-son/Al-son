I am a software engineer focused on **backend systems that stay reliable in production**. I own the work from domain modeling through deployment: data design, authentication and roles, PostgreSQL, API design, Docker, and the engineering choices that keep a codebase maintainable as the product grows.

I build with **Java and Kotlin (Spring Boot)** and **Python**. I have delivered platforms in education, healthcare, agriculture, commerce, media, and housing—environments where business rules, access control, and data integrity are central, not optional.

Alongside core backend engineering, I have a **strong background in machine learning and deep learning**. I train and evaluate models and integrate them into products through clear API contracts: classification, urgency detection, speech, and language-assisted features, with usage limits, logging, and behavior defined for production.
