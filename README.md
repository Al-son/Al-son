<h3 align="center">Backend Engineer · Applied AI</h3>

I design and ship server-side systems for real products: domain models, authentication, PostgreSQL, and services that stay maintainable after launch. My core is **Java and Kotlin with Spring Boot**. I also build with **Python and FastAPI** when the product calls for it.

I am taking that engineering into **applied AI** — intelligent features that belong inside the product and can be measured. The public example is **[YekoSkills](https://yekoskills.com)**, a platform for learning Python and AI. It covers courses, cohorts, roles, and **YekoCoach**, a tutor that answers from the lesson instead of acting as a generic chatbot.

### Now

- Backend APIs for education, operations, and digital products
- Applied AI on YekoSkills: contextual coaching, quotas, and a clear bar for answer quality
